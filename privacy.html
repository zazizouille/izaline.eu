Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Overview

Izaline Book Collection ("we", "us"), based in Copenhagen, Denmark, operates https://izaline.eu/ and the Facebook page Easy Danish Mysteries. We respect your privacy and adhere strictly to GDPR regulations.

2. Data We Collect

We only collect email addresses directly submitted by users for book release notifications. We do not sell, track, or share your data with third parties.

3. Meta API & Facebook Automation

Our automated posting tools use Meta's Graph API solely to publish Danish educational content to our public Facebook page. Our app does not harvest, store, or process any private Facebook user data.

4. Data Deletion & Contact

To request deletion of your email address or data, email us at contact@izaline.eu with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Requests are honored within 30 days.
