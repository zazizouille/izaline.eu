Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Overview

Izaline Book Collection ("we", "us", or "our"), based in Copenhagen, Denmark, operates https://izaline.eu/ and the Facebook page Easy Danish Mysteries. We are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy in full compliance with the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information directly provided by you, specifically:

  • Email Waitlist: When you subscribe to receive notifications regarding new book releases or audio companions, we collect your email address.
  • Inquiries: If you reach out to us directly via email (e.g., contact@izaline.eu), we retain your message and email to respond to your inquiry.
  • Social Media Interactions: When you interact with our official social media pages (e.g., Easy Danish Mysteries on Facebook or Instagram), we only process public interactions (comments, likes) provided through the respective platform's native tools.

3. Meta API & Facebook Page Automation

Our internal publishing system uses Meta's Graph API to publish educational Danish content, story carousels, and videos to our Facebook Page. Our application does not store, harvest, process, or sell any private user data, friends lists, or personal browsing profiles from Facebook users.

4. Data Deletion & User Rights

Under GDPR and international privacy regulations, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request the immediate deletion or correction of your email or subscription record.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To request data deletion or unsubscribe, email us at contact@izaline.eu with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". All associated data will be removed within 30 days.
